Privacy Policy

Doxora (Android app ID in.roypa.doxora) · Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Doxora mobile app ("Doxora", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Doxora converts images to PDF, PDF to Word (DOCX) and PDF to JPG. It is developed and published by ROY P A.

In short
  • Your documents and images are processed only on your device. We never upload, collect, view or store them on any server.
  • Doxora has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud storage, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and usage information to serve ads, as described below.

1. Files you convert

When you choose images or PDF files, Doxora opens them through Android's system file picker, so the app only gets access to the specific files you select. It does not request permission to read your storage as a whole.

All conversion happens on your device. Your files, their contents and the converted results are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Converted files are saved in the app's private storage on your device so you can find them later on the Files screen. Only Doxora can access this storage. You can delete files individually, delete all of them from Settings → Storage, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Temporary copies made while converting can be removed from Settings → Clear temporary files and are also cleared by Android over time.

When you choose Share or Save to device, the file is handed to the app or location you pick. What happens to it after that is governed by that app or service.

2. Settings stored on your device

Doxora stores your preferences (for example theme, default page size, image quality and favorite tools) locally on your device. These preferences are not sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Doxora is free and supported by banner ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may collect and process the following information. It sends this information to Google, not to us:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, ad measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud and security
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app interaction dataAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (for example SDK performance and crash data)Ad delivery and SDK reliability

The app may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad services (ad ID, attribution and topics APIs) where your device supports them. This data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can control Privacy Sandbox ad topics in Settings → Privacy → Ads → Ad privacy, where available.
  • You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads. Your files are never sent over the network.
  • Advertising ID and ad services (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*): used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for its internal operation.

Doxora does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or the whole of your device storage.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not have access to the files you convert.
  • We do not use our own analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Files and settings on your device stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall Doxora. Data that Google collects through AdMob is kept according to Google's own policies. You can manage it with the ad choices described above.

7. Children's privacy

Doxora is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help address it.

8. Security

Because conversions run on your device and your files stay in the app's private storage, your documents are not exposed to network transfer by Doxora. No method of storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or privacy laws in other regions), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Doxora itself holds no personal data about you. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your rights through Google's privacy controls. You can also contact us with any request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the app gains new features. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
